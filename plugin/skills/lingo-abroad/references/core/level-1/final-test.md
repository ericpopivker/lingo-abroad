# Level 1 · Final Test (shared spec)

About 12 minutes, or two halves of about 6. **Available once all six topic tests are passed** (if one isn't, say which and offer it instead). Follow `references/core/tests.md`: announce the whole test first, signpost every question ("Question 3 of 15"), **no feedback during the test** (after each answer only "Okay." or "Got it."), then the roleplay, then one review at the end.

**Announce:** "Level 1 final test: 15 questions from all six topics, then one longer scene, a day in <city>. Pass is 12 of 15 and polite answers in the scene. We can do it in two halves if you like."

**Two halves (optional):** Half 1 = questions 1–8. Half 2 = questions 9–15 + the roleplay. Between halves, put `Final test: half 1 done (<right>/8)` on the card's Tests line; Half 2 picks up from there in a new chat. Switching to anything else mid-half cancels only that half.

The correct answers are the target-language items taught for these meanings (use the forms on the card when they're there). Accept reasonable variants, any sight, city, number or time. Prompts with a sign or local line: say it in the target language. Topic codes: **Ba** Basics · **UH** Understanding & Getting Help · **Tr** Transport · **Si** Sightseeing · **Re** Restaurant · **Sh** Shopping; "Re L1·S1" = Restaurant, Lesson 1, Speak item S1.

## Roleplay: a day in <city> (polite register check)

"It's your first full day in <city>. I'll play everyone you meet." Set the scene once; then each turn is only the local's line, with a one-line **scene direction** in the learner's language when the place changes (never the phrase to say). Wait for each reply:

1. *Morning, a café.* Barista: greeting + "What would you like?" → Re L1·S2 ("I'd like a coffee, please").
2. Barista: "Here you are." → thank you (Ba L2·S2). *Later, you need the restroom.* → UH L3·S2.
3. *Outside, you're looking for the museum.* Passer-by: "Can I help you?" → Si L1·S1. Passer-by: "Straight ahead, then left. It's near here."
4. *At the museum desk.* Clerk: greeting + "Have you booked?" → Si L2·S2 (or "No" + two tickets).
5. *Afternoon, a small shop.* Shopkeeper: "Can I help you?" → Sh L3·S1 (just looking). *Later, you like a scarf.* → Sh L1·S3 (how much is it?). Shopkeeper: a price with cents.
6. → Sh L3·S3 (can I pay by card?). Shopkeeper: "Cash only, sorry." → OK (Ba L3·S5) + thank you.
7. *Evening, a restaurant.* Waiter: greeting + "Do you have a reservation?" → "No" + Re L1·S1 (a table for two).
8. *After dinner.* → Re L3·S2 (the bill, please). Waiter: "Cash or card?" → card (Re L3·S3) → thank you + goodbye (Ba L1·S3). Say "**Last one.**" before this turn.

Pass the scene if **all but two turns** were said on the learner's own (no hint, no English; small slips are fine) **and** every reply is polite (no commands or casual forms like Italian _Ciao_, _Dammi_, _Puoi_). Turns that needed help go to Weak.

## Result

Say the score. **Pass = at least 12/15 AND the scene.** On pass, add **"Level 1 Passed ✓ <score>/15"** to the card's Tests line and name the skill in one sentence ("You can now get through a whole day in <the country> politely: cafés, trains, museums, shops and dinner!"); then offer Level 2 when it's ready, or practice and freestyle meanwhile. On fail: the score, one sentence on what went well, then only the misses (right answer + memory trick, one sentence each); missed items go to **Weak**; say which topic to practice before retaking. Retakes are always open.
