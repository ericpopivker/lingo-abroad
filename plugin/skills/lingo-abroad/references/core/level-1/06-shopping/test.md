# Level 1 · Shopping · Topic Test (shared spec)

About 7 minutes. **10 items, one at a time, no hints.** The learner answers; reply only "Okay." or "Got it." and ask the next one: **no praise or corrections until the end.** Then the roleplay. Follow `references/core/tests.md`.

The correct answers are the target-language items you taught for these meanings (use the forms on the card when they're there). Accept reasonable variants and any correct number. Prompts with a sign or local line: say it in the target language. "L1·S1" = Lesson 1, Speak item S1.

## Items

| # | Type | Prompt (you say) | Correct answer |
| :- | :--- | :--------------- | :------------- |
| 1 | Speak | "Count from one to five." | L1·S1 (all five, at most one slip) |
| 2 | Speak | "Say: seven, nine, six." (in English; one at a time) | the three numbers from L1·S2 |
| 3 | Listen | "The seller says [Fifty euros]. How much?" | €50 |
| 4 | Listen | "The clerk says [Eight fifty]. How much?" | €8.50 |
| 5 | Speak | "Point at the pastries and ask for three." | L1·S4 |
| 6 | Speak | "You need cash. Ask a passer-by if there's an ATM near here." | L2·S3 |
| 7 | Read | "A café door says [Pull]. Push or pull?" | Pull |
| 8 | Speak | "A shopkeeper asks [Can I help you?]. You're only browsing." | L3·S1 |
| 9 | Read | "A sign at the market stall says [Cash only]. Can you pay by card?" | No, cash only |
| 10 | Listen | "At the bakery you hear [For here or to go?]. What are they asking?" | For here or to go? |

[Brackets] = say it in the target language, not in English.

**After the scene:** the score, **one sentence on what went well** (no list of correct answers), then one review of **only** the missed items and scene turns that needed help (right answer + memory trick, one sentence each). Missed items go to **Weak**.

## Roleplay (polite register check)

"A morning of shopping in <city>. I'll play each person." Wait for each reply:

1. Small shop: greeting + "Can I help you?" (L3·L1) → L2·S1 (do you have sunscreen?). Shopkeeper: "Yes, over there." (L2·L1)
2. → L1·S3 (how much is it?). Shopkeeper: a price with cents (L1·L2) → learner says it back to check.
3. → L3·S2 (I'll take it) + L3·S3 (can I pay by card?). Shopkeeper: "The card machine isn't working." (L3·L3) → learner: OK + pays cash.
4. Shopkeeper: "Do you need a bag?" → L3·S4 (no, thank you) + thank you, goodbye.

Pass the scene if **all but one turn** was said on the learner's own (no hint, no English; small slips and a number corrected on the spot are fine) **and** every reply is polite (no commands or casual forms like Italian _Dammi_, _Puoi_, _Ciao_). Turns that needed help go to Weak.

## Result

Say the score. On pass, add **"Shopping Passed ✓ <score>/10"** to the card's Tests line and name the skill ("You can now count, find what you need and pay anywhere in <the country>!"). On fail, say which lesson to redo (the one with the most missed items) and that retakes are always open.
