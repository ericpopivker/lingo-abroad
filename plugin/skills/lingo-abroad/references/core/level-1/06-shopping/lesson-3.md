# Level 1 · Shopping · Lesson 3: Paying & Deciding (shared spec)

**Goal:** browse without pressure, decide to buy, pay by card or cash, and spot "cash only" before you order. About 7 minutes.

This spec lists **meanings**, not words. Write each item in the target language as a polite local would say it in the destination country (see SKILL.md, Writing the items).

## Speak — what you say

| # | Meaning | When | Target-language note |
| :- | :------ | :--- | :------------------- |
| S1 | "Just looking, thank you." | The shopkeeper greets you and offers help | Polite and friendly, not dismissive. |
| S2 | "I'll take it." | You decide to buy | |
| S3 | "Can I pay by card?" | Before paying | Quick recall if known from Restaurant or Transport. |
| S4 | "No, thank you." (to a bag, a receipt, anything offered) | Paying | Quick recall from Basics if known. |

## Listen — what locals say back

| # | Meaning | Situation |
| :- | :------ | :-------- |
| L1 | "Can I help you?" | Shopkeeper as you walk in (the cue for S1) |
| L2 | "For here or to go?" | Bakeries, cafés, takeaway pizza |
| L3 | "The card machine isn't working." / "Cash only." | Paying in small shops, markets, some taxis |

## Read — what you'll see

| # | Meaning | Where |
| :- | :------ | :---- |
| R1 | Cash only | Small cafés, markets, kiosks (e.g. Italy: _Solo contanti_) |
| R2 | Fitting rooms | Clothes shops |
| R3 | Price per kilo | Market stalls, delis (e.g. Italy: € / kg, _al chilo_) |

## Roleplay — 3 short scenes

1. **Clothes shop:** "You walk in. Shopkeeper: greeting + L1." → S1. Shopkeeper: "Of course, take your time." Later: you like a scarf → S2. Shopkeeper: "Do you need a bag?" → S4.
2. **Paying:** → S3. Shopkeeper: L3 (card machine isn't working) → learner: "OK" (Basics) + pays cash, thank you.
3. **Bakery:** "You order a slice of pizza (point: 'this one, please'). Baker: L2." → learner answers "To go, please" or "For here, please" (give both once if needed). Then: "A sign by the till says <R1>. What does it mean?"
