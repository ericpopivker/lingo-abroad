# Level 1 · Restaurant · Topic Test (shared spec)

About 7 minutes. **10 items, one at a time, no hints.** The learner answers; reply only "Okay." or "Got it." and ask the next one: **no praise or corrections until the end.** Then the roleplay. Follow `references/core/tests.md`.

The correct answers are the target-language items you taught for these meanings (use the forms on the card when they're there). Prompts with a sign or local line: say it in the target language. "L1·S1" = Lesson 1, Speak item S1.

## Items

| # | Type | Prompt (you say) | Correct answer |
| :- | :--- | :--------------- | :------------- |
| 1 | Speak | "You walk into a restaurant with a friend. Ask for a table." | L1·S1 (table for two) |
| 2 | Listen | "The waiter asks [Do you have a reservation?]. What did he ask?" | Do you have a reservation? |
| 3 | Speak | "Order a coffee politely." | L1·S2 + coffee ("I'd like a coffee") |
| 4 | Read | "A menu heading says [Starters]. What's in that section?" | Starters |
| 5 | Speak | "You point at a dish you don't know. What do you ask?" | L2·S1 (What is this?) |
| 6 | Speak | "You can't decide. Ask the waiter for advice." | L2·S2 (What do you recommend?) |
| 7 | Listen | "After you mention an allergy, the waiter says [I'll check with the kitchen]. What did he say?" | I'll check with the kitchen |
| 8 | Read | "The board outside says [Dish of the day]. What does it mean?" | Dish of the day / today's special |
| 9 | Speak | "You're finished and ready to pay. What do you say?" | L3·S2 (The bill, please) |
| 10 | Listen | "The waiter asks [Cash or card?]. What did he ask?" | Cash or card? |

[Brackets] = say it in the target language, not in English.

**After the scene:** the score, **one sentence on what went well** (no list of correct answers), then one review of **only** the missed items and scene turns that needed help (right answer + memory trick, one sentence each). Missed items go to **Weak**.

## Roleplay (polite register check)

"You're at a trattoria at eight in the evening. I'm the waiter." Play these turns, waiting for each reply:

1. Waiter greets you → learner: greeting + table for two (L1·S1).
2. Waiter: "What are you having?" (L1·L2) → learner: "I'd like…" + a dish (L1·S2).
3. Waiter: "Still or sparkling?" (L1·L3) → learner: still water, please (L1·S3).
4. Later, waiter: "Is everything all right?" (L3·L1) → learner: it's delicious (L3·S1).
5. Learner asks for the bill (L3·S2); waiter: "Cash or card?" (L3·L2) → learner: card (L3·S3).

Pass the scene if **all but one turn** was said on the learner's own (no hint, no English; small slips are fine) **and** every reply is polite (no "I want…", no casual forms). Turns that needed help go to Weak.

## Result

Say the score. On pass, add **"Restaurant Passed ✓ <score>/10"** to the card's Tests line and name the skill ("You can now get a table, order, ask about dishes and pay in any restaurant in <the country>!"). On fail, say which lesson to redo and that retakes are always open.
