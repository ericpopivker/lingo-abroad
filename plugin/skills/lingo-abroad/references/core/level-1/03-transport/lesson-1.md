# Level 1 · Transport · Lesson 1: Taxi (shared spec)

**Goal:** get from the airport or station into town by taxi: give the address, ask the price, pay, and stop where you want. About 7 minutes.

This spec lists **meanings**, not words. Write each item in the target language as a polite local would say it in the destination country (see SKILL.md, Writing the items).

## Speak — what you say

| # | Meaning | When | Target-language note |
| :- | :------ | :--- | :------------------- |
| S1 | "Can you take me to this address, please?" | Getting in; show the address on your phone | Polite request form, not a command. |
| S2 | "How much is it to the center?" | **Before** the ride starts | |
| S3 | "Stop here, please." | You see your hotel | Polite form. |
| S4 | "Can I pay by card?" | Before or at the end | If the learner already knows it from Restaurant, it's a quick recall, not a new item. |

## Listen — what locals say back

| # | Meaning | Situation |
| :- | :------ | :-------- |
| L1 | "Where are you going?" | The driver, as you get in |
| L2 | "Cash or card?" | Paying |
| L3 | "We've arrived." / "Here we are." | At the destination |

## Read — what you'll see

| # | Meaning | Where |
| :- | :------ | :---- |
| R1 | Taxi rank / Taxi | Airport and station exits |
| R2 | Arrivals | Airport and station boards |
| R3 | Departures | Airport and station boards |
| R4 | City center | Road and station signs |

**Local taxi note (one sentence, once):** how taxis work in the country (e.g. Italy: only from the official rank or by phone or app; drivers don't stop on the street).

## Roleplay — 3 short scenes

1. **At the rank:** "You arrive at the main station and get into a taxi. Driver: greeting + L1." → learner shows the address: S1.
2. **The price:** "Before he starts, ask the price to the center." → S2. Driver gives a price + L2 → learner: S4. Driver: "Yes, of course."
3. **Arriving:** "You see your hotel on the right." → S3. Driver: L3 → learner: thank you + goodbye (Basics).
