# Level 1 · Transport · Topic Test (shared spec)

About 7 minutes. **10 items, one at a time, no hints.** The learner answers; reply only "Okay." or "Got it." and ask the next one: **no praise or corrections until the end.** Then the roleplay. Follow `references/core/tests.md`.

The correct answers are the target-language items you taught for these meanings (use the forms on the card when they're there). Accept reasonable variants (any destination or city). Prompts with a sign or local line: say it in the target language. "L1·S1" = Lesson 1, Speak item S1.

## Items

| # | Type | Prompt (you say) | Correct answer |
| :- | :--- | :--------------- | :------------- |
| 1 | Speak | "You get into a taxi and show your hotel address. What do you say?" | L1·S1 (take me to this address, please) |
| 2 | Read | "At the airport, a sign says [Taxi rank]. What is it?" | Taxi rank |
| 3 | Speak | "Before the taxi starts, ask how much it is to the center." | L1·S2 |
| 4 | Listen | "The driver asks [Cash or card?]. What does he want to know?" | Cash or card? |
| 5 | Speak | "You're at the shop that sells tickets. Ask for one ticket." | L2·S1 |
| 6 | Read | "A machine on the bus says [Validate your ticket]. What should you do?" | Validate / stamp the ticket (or the country's equivalent) |
| 7 | Speak | "On the bus, ask where you should get off." | L2·S4 |
| 8 | Listen | "The ticket clerk asks [One-way or return?]. What is she asking?" | One-way or return? |
| 9 | Speak | "On the platform, check with a conductor that this is the train to <city>." | L3·S3 |
| 10 | Read | "The departure board says [Delayed]. What does it mean?" | Delayed |

[Brackets] = say it in the target language, not in English.

**After the scene:** the score, **one sentence on what went well** (no list of correct answers), then one review of **only** the missed items and scene turns that needed help (right answer + memory trick, one sentence each). Missed items go to **Weak**.

## Roleplay (polite register check)

"You're at the main station and need to get to <city>, then a taxi at the other end. I'll play each person." Wait for each reply:

1. Ticket clerk: greeting + "What would you like?" → L3·S1 (return ticket).
2. Learner asks for the platform (L3·S2). Clerk: "Platform <number>."
3. In <city>, taxi driver: greeting + "Where are you going?" (L1·L1) → L1·S1.
4. Near the hotel → L1·S3 (stop here). Driver: "We've arrived." (L1·L3) → L1·S4 (pay by card) or thank you + goodbye.

Pass the scene if **all but one turn** was said on the learner's own (no hint, no English; small slips are fine) **and** every reply is polite (no commands or casual forms like Italian _Portami_, _Fermati_, _Puoi_, _Ciao_). Turns that needed help go to Weak.

## Result

Say the score. On pass, add **"Transport Passed ✓ <score>/10"** to the card's Tests line and name the skill ("You can now take a taxi, a bus, the metro and the train anywhere in <the country>!"). On fail, say which lesson to redo and that retakes are always open.
