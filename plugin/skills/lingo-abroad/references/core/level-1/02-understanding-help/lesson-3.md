# Level 1 · Understanding & Getting Help · Lesson 3: Getting Help (shared spec)

**Goal:** find the restroom and the pharmacy, call for help, and ask for the police or a doctor. About 7 minutes. Teach calmly: the emergency phrases are the ones you hope never to need; the restroom one you'll use every day.

This spec lists **meanings**, not words. Write each item in the target language as a polite local would say it in the destination country (see SKILL.md, Writing the items).

**Safety:** emergency phrases and numbers must be checked against an official source before this lesson is marked verified. Facts used: **112** is the emergency number across the EU (police, ambulance, fire). For a country outside the EU, use its official number; if you're not sure of it, say so instead of guessing.

## Speak — what you say

| # | Meaning | When | Target-language note |
| :- | :------ | :--- | :------------------- |
| S1 | "Excuse me, where is the pharmacy?" | Minor illness, you need medicine | Reuse "excuse me" from Basics. |
| S2 | "Excuse me, where is the restroom?" | Every day: cafés, museums, stations | Same "where is…?" as S1 with one word swapped: teach it **right after S1** as a quick variant ("Same start, new word"), then do the usual recall. |
| S3 | "Help!" | Emergency: shout it | |
| S4 | "Call the police, please!" | Theft, danger | Polite request form even in an emergency. |
| S5 | "I need a doctor." | Illness or injury | |

**Number to remember (say once, in the learner's language):** the country's emergency number (e.g. "In Italy, dial one-one-two").

## Listen — what locals say back

| # | Meaning | Situation |
| :- | :------ | :-------- |
| L1 | "It's over there, on the right." | Answering S1 or S2 |
| L2 | "What happened?" | Someone comes to help |
| L3 | "They're coming right away." | After calling for help |

## Read — what you'll see

| # | Meaning | Where |
| :- | :------ | :---- |
| R1 | Emergency room | Hospitals (e.g. Italy: _Pronto soccorso_) |
| R2 | Pharmacy | Shop sign (e.g. Italy and most of Europe: a green cross) |
| R3 | On-duty pharmacy (open at night / on holidays) | Notice on pharmacy doors, where the country has one (e.g. Italy: _Farmacia di turno_) |
| R4 | Police | Station signs, cars (e.g. Italy: _Polizia_ and _Carabinieri_, both police) |

## Roleplay — 3 short scenes

Keep the tone calm and supportive.

1. **At a café:** "You've finished your coffee and need the restroom. Ask the barista." → S2. Barista: L1 → learner: thank you. Then: "That evening you have a headache. You stop someone in the street." → S1. Local: L1.
2. **A fall:** "Your friend falls in the street and hurts their leg. A passer-by comes over: L2." → learner: S3 + S5. Passer-by: L3.
3. **Theft:** "Someone just grabbed a bag at the café. The owner looks at you." → learner: S4. Owner: L3.
