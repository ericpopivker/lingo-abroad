# Level 1 · Understanding & Getting Help · Lesson 3: Asking for Help (shared spec)

**Goal:** ask a stranger for help, find the restroom (and the pharmacy), and find an address. About 7 minutes. Everyday help only: this course doesn't teach emergency phrases.

This spec lists **meanings**, not words. Write each item in the target language as a polite local would say it in the destination country (see SKILL.md, Writing the items).

## Speak — what you say

| # | Meaning | When | Target-language note |
| :- | :------ | :--- | :------------------- |
| S1 | "Excuse me, can you help me?" | Stopping a stranger or asking staff | Reuse "excuse me" from Basics; polite form. |
| S2 | "Where is the restroom?" | Every day: cafés, museums, stations | After the learner repeats it, mention once, without a new "Your turn": "Same for the pharmacy" + the phrase (e.g. Italian _Dov'è la farmacia?_). |
| S3 | "I'm looking for this address." (showing your phone) | On the way to your hotel or a sight | Without a phone, the same start works with a place ("I'm looking for the station"). |

## Listen — what locals say back

| # | Meaning | Situation |
| :- | :------ | :-------- |
| L1 | "Of course. How can I help?" | Answering S1 |
| L2 | "It's over there." (pointing) | Answering S2 (or the pharmacy question). No left / right yet: directions come in Sightseeing. |
| L3 | "It's near here, that way." (pointing) | Answering S3 |

## Read — what you'll see

| # | Meaning | Where |
| :- | :------ | :---- |
| R1 | Pharmacy | Shop sign (e.g. Italy and most of Europe: a green cross) |
| R2 | On-duty pharmacy (open at night / on holidays) | Notice on pharmacy doors, where the country has one (e.g. Italy: _Farmacia di turno_) |
| R3 | Lost property | Stations, airports, museums |

## Roleplay — 3 short scenes

1. **At a café:** "You've finished your coffee and need the restroom. Ask the barista." → "excuse me" + S2. Barista: L2 → learner: thank you.
2. **A headache:** "That evening you have a headache. You stop someone in the street." → S1. Local: L1 → learner asks where the pharmacy is (the form mentioned after S2). Local: L2.
3. **Finding the hotel:** "You're looking for your hotel and the street names don't match your map." → S3 (showing the phone). Local: L3 → learner: thank you very much.
