# Level 1 · Understanding & Getting Help · Lesson 3: Asking for Help (shared spec)

**Goal:** ask a stranger for help, find the restroom and the pharmacy, and get back on track when you're lost. About 7 minutes. Everyday help only: this course doesn't teach emergency phrases.

This spec lists **meanings**, not words. Write each item in the target language as a polite local would say it in the destination country (see SKILL.md, Writing the items).

## Speak — what you say

| # | Meaning | When | Target-language note |
| :- | :------ | :--- | :------------------- |
| S1 | "Excuse me, can you help me?" | Stopping a stranger or asking staff | Reuse "excuse me" from Basics; polite form. |
| S2 | "Where is the restroom?" | Every day: cafés, museums, stations | |
| S3 | "Where is the pharmacy?" | You need something for a headache or a cold | Same "where is…?" as S2 with one word swapped: teach it **right after S2** as a quick variant ("Same start, new word"), then do the usual recall. |
| S4 | "I'm lost. I'm looking for this address." (showing your phone) | Lost on the way to your hotel or a sight | |

## Listen — what locals say back

| # | Meaning | Situation |
| :- | :------ | :-------- |
| L1 | "Of course. How can I help?" | Answering S1 |
| L2 | "It's over there, on the right." | Answering S2 or S3 |
| L3 | "It's near here: second street on the left." | Answering S4 |

## Read — what you'll see

| # | Meaning | Where |
| :- | :------ | :---- |
| R1 | Pharmacy | Shop sign (e.g. Italy and most of Europe: a green cross) |
| R2 | On-duty pharmacy (open at night / on holidays) | Notice on pharmacy doors, where the country has one (e.g. Italy: _Farmacia di turno_) |
| R3 | Lost property | Stations, airports, museums |

## Roleplay — 3 short scenes

1. **At a café:** "You've finished your coffee and need the restroom. Ask the barista." → "excuse me" + S2. Barista: L2 → learner: thank you.
2. **A headache:** "That evening you have a headache. You stop someone in the street." → S1. Local: L1 → learner: S3. Local: L2.
3. **Lost:** "You're looking for your hotel and the street names don't match your map." → S4 (showing the phone). Local: L3 → learner: thank you very much.
