# Level 1 · Understanding & Getting Help · Lesson 2: Working It Out (shared spec)

**Goal:** get unstuck on your own: tell people you speak only a little of the language, ask how to say something, and ask what a word means. About 7 minutes.

This spec lists **meanings**, not words. Write each item in the target language as a polite local would say it in the destination country (see SKILL.md, Writing the items).

## Speak — what you say

| # | Meaning | When | Target-language note |
| :- | :------ | :--- | :------------------- |
| S1 | "I speak only a little <language>." | When a local starts talking fast or at length; it makes them slow down and keep it simple | The most natural everyday form (e.g. Italian _Parlo solo un po' d'italiano_). Better than "I don't speak <language>", which ends the conversation. |
| S2 | "How do you say … in <language>?" | You know the word in your language but not in theirs | Practice with "water" first; the learner fills the gap with any word. |
| S3 | "What does it mean?" | You see or hear a word you don't know | |

## Listen — what locals say back

| # | Meaning | Situation |
| :- | :------ | :-------- |
| L1 | "You say '<word>'." | Answering S2 (e.g. "You say 'water'" in the language) |
| L2 | "It means '<word>'." | Answering S3 |
| L3 | "No problem, I'll speak slowly." | Answering S1 |

## Read — what you'll see

| # | Meaning | Where |
| :- | :------ | :---- |
| R1 | Emergency exit | Doors in stations, hotels, museums |
| R2 | Closed for holidays | Shop doors (common in summer and around holidays) |
| R3 | Roadworks / Work in progress | Streets, station passages |
| R4 | Opening hours / Timetable | Shop doors, museums, stops |

## Roleplay — 3 short scenes

1. **At a kiosk:** "You want water but don't know the word. Vendor: greeting + 'What would you like?'" → learner: S2 (water). Vendor: L1 → learner: "This one, please" (Basics) or the new word + please.
2. **Too fast:** the vendor starts chatting quickly about the weather → learner: S1. Vendor: L3, then says the price slowly → learner: thank you.
3. **A sign:** "Next door, a shop sign says <R2>." → learner asks the vendor: S3 about the word for "holidays". Vendor: L2.
