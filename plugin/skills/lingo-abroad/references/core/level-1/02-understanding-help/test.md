# Level 1 · Understanding & Getting Help · Topic Test (shared spec)

About 7 minutes. **10 items, one at a time, no hints.** The learner answers; reply only "Okay." or "Got it." and ask the next one: **no praise or corrections until the end.** Then the roleplay. Follow `references/core/tests.md`.

The correct answers are the target-language items you taught for these meanings (use the forms on the card when they're there). Accept reasonable variants. Prompts with a sign or local line: say it in the target language. "L1·S1" = Lesson 1, Speak item S1.

## Items

| # | Type | Prompt (you say) | Correct answer |
| :- | :--- | :--------------- | :------------- |
| 1 | Speak | "A clerk answers you fast and you're lost. What do you say?" | L1·S1 (I don't understand) |
| 2 | Read | "You see a sign that says [Restrooms]. What does it mean?" | Restrooms / Toilets |
| 3 | Speak | "Before switching to English, politely ask if they speak it." | L1·S2 |
| 4 | Read | "A restroom door says [Men]. Who is it for?" | Men (if the country has a look-alike pair, use the trap word, e.g. Italy: _Signori_) |
| 5 | Speak | "A shopkeeper starts chatting fast. Tell her you speak only a little of the language." | L2·S1 |
| 6 | Listen | "You ask a question and hear [Pardon?]. What do they mean?" | Pardon? / What? |
| 7 | Speak | "You're in a museum and need the restroom. Ask a guard." | L3·S2 (excuse me, where is the restroom?) |
| 8 | Read | "A shop door says [Closed for holidays]. What does it mean?" | Closed for holidays |
| 9 | Speak | "You're lost on the way to your hotel. Show your phone and say so." | L3·S4 |
| 10 | Listen | "You ask for help and hear [Of course. How can I help?]. What did they say?" | Of course, how can I help? |

[Brackets] = say it in the target language, not in English.

**After the scene:** the score, **one sentence on what went well** (no list of correct answers), then one review of **only** the missed items and scene turns that needed help (right answer + memory trick, one sentence each). Missed items go to **Weak**.

## Roleplay (polite register check)

"You're at a hotel reception late at night and feel unwell. I'm the night clerk." Wait for each reply:

1. The clerk greets you and says something fast in the language. → "excuse me" + L1·S2. Clerk: L1·L1 (a little).
2. The clerk explains something quickly again. → L1·S3 or L1·S4.
3. The learner needs medicine → L3·S3 (where is the pharmacy?). Clerk: "The on-duty pharmacy is nearby" (L3·R2 word, or "a pharmacy is nearby").
4. The clerk explains the way quickly → L2·S1 (only a little). Clerk: L2·L3, then says it slowly → learner: thank you.

Pass the scene if **all but one turn** was said on the learner's own (no hint, no English; small slips are fine) **and** every reply is polite (no commands or casual forms like Italian _Parli_, _Puoi_, _Ciao_, _Ehi_). Turns that needed help go to Weak.

## Result

Say the score. On pass, add **"Understanding & Help Passed ✓ <score>/10"** to the card's Tests line and name the skill ("You can now get unstuck, find the restroom and ask for help anywhere in <the country>!"). On fail, say which lesson to redo (the one with the most missed items) and that retakes are always open.
