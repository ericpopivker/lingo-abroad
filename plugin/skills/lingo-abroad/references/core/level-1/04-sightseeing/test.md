# Level 1 · Sightseeing · Topic Test (shared spec)

About 7 minutes. **10 items, one at a time, no hints.** The learner answers; reply only "Okay." or "Got it." and ask the next one: **no praise or corrections until the end.** Then the roleplay. Follow `references/core/tests.md`.

The correct answers are the target-language items you taught for these meanings (use the forms on the card when they're there). Accept reasonable variants (any sight, number or time). Prompts with a sign or local line: say it in the target language. "L1·S1" = Lesson 1, Speak item S1.

## Items

| # | Type | Prompt (you say) | Correct answer |
| :- | :--- | :--------------- | :------------- |
| 1 | Speak | "Stop someone and ask where <a famous sight> is." | L1·S1 |
| 2 | Listen | "They answer [Straight ahead, then left]. Which way?" | Straight on, then left |
| 3 | Speak | "Ask if it's far." | L1·S2 |
| 4 | Read | "A sign at the edge of the old town says [Pedestrian zone]. What does it mean?" | Pedestrian zone (no cars) |
| 5 | Speak | "At the museum desk, show your phone: you booked online." | L2·S2 |
| 6 | Speak | "Ask what time the museum opens." | L2·S3 |
| 7 | Read | "The website says [Booking required]. What does it mean?" | You must book in advance |
| 8 | Speak | "In a church, ask the guard if you can take a photo." | L3·S1 |
| 9 | Listen | "The guard says [No, it's not allowed]. Can you?" | No |
| 10 | Read | "A sign at the church door says [Shoulders and knees covered]. What does it mean?" | Dress code: cover shoulders and knees |

[Brackets] = say it in the target language, not in English.

**After the scene:** the score, **one sentence on what went well** (no list of correct answers), then one review of **only** the missed items and scene turns that needed help (right answer + memory trick, one sentence each). Missed items go to **Weak**.

## Roleplay (polite register check)

"A day of sightseeing in <city>. I'll play each person." Wait for each reply:

1. You're lost near the station; a passer-by → "excuse me" + L1·S1. Passer-by: L1·L1 + L1·L2 → learner: L1·S2. Passer-by: L1·L3.
2. At the museum desk: greeting + "Have you booked?" (L2·L1) → "No" + L2·S1 (two tickets). Clerk gives a price → learner: L2·S4 (audio guide).
3. Inside, you want a photo of the two of you → "excuse me" + L3·S2. Visitor: L3·L3.
4. Leaving: → L3·S3 (where is the exit?). Guard: "Straight ahead." → learner: thank you, goodbye.

Pass the scene if **all but one turn** was said on the learner's own (no hint, no English; small slips are fine) **and** every reply is polite (no commands or casual forms like Italian _Fammi_, _Puoi_, _Ciao_). Turns that needed help go to Weak.

## Result

Say the score. On pass, add **"Sightseeing Passed ✓ <score>/10"** to the card's Tests line and name the skill ("You can now find your way, get into any museum and follow the rules inside, anywhere in <the country>!"). On fail, say which lesson to redo (the one with the most missed items) and that retakes are always open.
