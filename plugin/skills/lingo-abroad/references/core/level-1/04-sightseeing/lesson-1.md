# Level 1 · Sightseeing · Lesson 1: Finding Your Way (shared spec)

**Goal:** ask where a sight is, whether it's far, and to be shown on the map; understand "straight ahead", "right", "left" and "it's near here". About 7 minutes.

This spec lists **meanings**, not words. Write each item in the target language as a polite local would say it in the destination country (see SKILL.md, Writing the items).

## Speak — what you say

| # | Meaning | When | Target-language note |
| :- | :------ | :--- | :------------------- |
| S1 | "Excuse me, where is <sight>?" | Any sight, street or square | Use a famous sight in the destination city. If the learner knows "where is…?" from Understanding & Help (pharmacy, restroom), it's a quick recall with a new place. |
| S2 | "Is it far?" | Deciding whether to walk | |
| S3 | "Can you show me on the map?" | Holding out your phone or a paper map | Polite form. |

## Listen — what locals say back

| # | Meaning | Situation |
| :- | :------ | :-------- |
| L1 | "Straight ahead." | Directions |
| L2 | "To the right." / "To the left." | Directions; teach as a pair |
| L3 | "No, it's near here." | Answering S2 |

## Read — what you'll see

| # | Meaning | Where |
| :- | :------ | :---- |
| R1 | Street (on street-name signs) | Building corners (e.g. Italy: _Via Roma_) |
| R2 | Square | Street-name signs and maps (e.g. Italy: _Piazza_) |
| R3 | Historic center / Old town | Road signs into town |
| R4 | Pedestrian zone | Street entrances (cars not allowed) |

## Roleplay — 3 short scenes

1. **Lost near the station:** "You stop a woman with shopping bags." → S1. Woman: L1 + "then" + L2 (right).
2. **Walk or not:** → S2. Woman: L3 → learner: thank you.
3. **The map:** "A man at a newsstand. You want to see it on your phone map." → S3. Man: "Of course… here, " + L2 (left) → learner: thank you very much. **(Extras on)** curveball: he gives the directions fast; the learner asks him to repeat (Understanding & Help).
