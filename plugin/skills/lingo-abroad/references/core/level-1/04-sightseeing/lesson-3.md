# Level 1 · Sightseeing · Lesson 3: At the Sight (shared spec)

**Goal:** ask if photos are allowed, ask someone to take your picture, find the exit, follow church and museum rules, and say how beautiful it is. About 7 minutes.

This spec lists **meanings**, not words. Write each item in the target language as a polite local would say it in the destination country (see SKILL.md, Writing the items).

## Speak — what you say

| # | Meaning | When | Target-language note |
| :- | :------ | :--- | :------------------- |
| S1 | "Can I take a photo?" | Inside churches and museums: ask first | |
| S2 | "Could you take a photo of us, please?" | Handing a stranger your phone | Polite form. |
| S3 | "Excuse me, where is the exit?" | Big museums are mazes | Same "where is…?" as Lesson 1: a quick swap. |
| S4 | "How beautiful!" | Anytime; locals love hearing it | |

## Listen — what locals say back

| # | Meaning | Situation |
| :- | :------ | :-------- |
| L1 | "Yes, but without flash." | Answering S1 |
| L2 | "No, it's not allowed." | Answering S1 |
| L3 | "Of course! Smile!" | Answering S2 (to two or more people, if the language has a plural form) |

## Read — what you'll see

| # | Meaning | Where |
| :- | :------ | :---- |
| R1 | No photography | Museum rooms, churches |
| R2 | Silence | Churches, chapels |
| R3 | Shoulders and knees covered (dress code) | Church and Vatican entrances |
| R4 | Cloakroom / Bag check | Museum entrances (big bags often must be left) |

**Local note (one sentence, once):** dress or behavior rules that catch visitors out (e.g. Italy: churches and the Vatican expect shoulders and knees covered; carry a light scarf).

## Roleplay — 3 short scenes

1. **In a church:** "You want a photo of the ceiling. A guard stands nearby." → "excuse me" + S1. Guard: L1 → learner: thank you. Then: "In the next room a sign says <R1>. What does it mean?"
2. **On the square:** "You want a picture of you and your friend in front of the fountain. A woman walks by." → "excuse me" + S2. Woman: L3 → learner: thank you very much + S4.
3. **Leaving the museum:** "You can't find the way out." → S3. Guard: "Straight ahead, then left" (Lesson 1) → learner: thank you, goodbye.
