# Level 1 · Basics · Topic Test (shared spec)

About 7 minutes. **10 items, one at a time, no hints.** The learner answers; reply only "Okay." or "Got it." and ask the next one: **no praise or corrections until the end.** Then the roleplay.

**Pass = at least 8/10 right AND the scene (all but one turn on your own, every reply polite).** Accept small typos and missing accents in text; don't grade pronunciation. An informal form in the roleplay fails the roleplay part.

The correct answers are the target-language items you taught for these meanings (use the forms on the card when they're there). Prompts with a sign or local line: say it in the target language.

## Items

Ask in this order (mixes Speak, Listen and Read across all three lessons). "L1·S1" = Lesson 1, Speak item S1.

| # | Type | Prompt (you say) | Correct answer |
| :- | :--- | :--------------- | :------------- |
| 1 | Speak | "You walk into a shop at ten in the morning. What do you say?" | L1·S1 (daytime hello) |
| 2 | Read | "A sign on the door says [Closed]. What does it mean?" | Closed |
| 3 | Speak | "You point at a pastry. How do you ask for it?" | L2·S1 ("This one, please") |
| 4 | Listen | "The barista hands you your coffee and says [Here you are]. What did she mean?" | Here you are |
| 5 | Speak | "You need to squeeze past people on a crowded bus. What do you say?" | L3·S4 (or S3 if the language uses one phrase) |
| 6 | Read | "A ticket machine has a sign: [Out of order]. What does it mean?" | Out of order / broken |
| 7 | Speak | "Someone thanks you for holding the door. How do you reply?" | L2·S3 ("You're welcome") |
| 8 | Listen | "You ask for the restroom and hear [I'm sorry, it's closed]. What did they say?" | I'm sorry, it's closed |
| 9 | Speak | "It's seven in the evening, you enter a restaurant. What do you say?" | L1·S2 (evening hello, or the all-day hello) |
| 10 | Read | "A sign says [No smoking]. What does it mean?" | No smoking |

[Brackets] = say it in the target language, not in English.

**After the scene:** the score, **one sentence on what went well** (no list of correct answers), then one review of **only** the missed items and scene turns that needed help (right answer + memory trick, one sentence each). Missed items go to **Weak**.

## Roleplay (polite register check)

"You're in a café. I'm the waiter." Play these turns, waiting for each reply:

1. The waiter is across the room. → learner gets attention: L3·S3.
2. Waiter: "Of course! What would you like?" (L3·L2 + L2·L1) → learner points at the menu: L2·S1.
3. Waiter: "Here you are." (L2·L2) → learner: L2·S2 (thank you).
4. The learner leaves. Waiter: "Have a nice day!" (L1·L2) → learner: L1·S3 (goodbye).

Pass the scene if **all but one turn** was said on the learner's own (no hint, no English; small slips are fine) **and** every reply is polite (no casual greetings, goodbyes or "you" forms). Turns that needed help go to Weak.

## Result

Say the score ("9 out of 10, and you handled the café scene on your own: you passed Basics!"). On pass, add **"Basics Passed ✓ <score>/10"** to the card's Tests line. On fail, say which lesson to redo (the one with the most missed items) and that retakes are always open.
