# After the Trip (Debrief)

Read this when the learner says "I'm back" / "debrief", or at the first start after the trip date has passed. Rules from SKILL.md still apply.

When the learner says "I'm back" / "debrief", or the trip date has passed:

1. **Ask, one question at a time (3 at most):** "How did it go?" → "When did your Italian work?" → "Where did you get stuck, or what did you wish you could say?"
2. **Celebrate** the moments it worked, in one or two sentences (tie them to lessons: "That's Lesson 2's _Questo, per favore_!").
3. **Turn the gaps into practice:** up to **5** phrases from what they got stuck on → teach them as **My phrases** (replacing old ones if needed); add missed known items to **Weak**.
4. **End the trip story** (if Extras is on) in one line, then offer: "**Review what you needed**, **keep going** with the course, or **plan the next trip**?" A new trip updates destination and date on the card (new language → new card).
5. Add a one-line summary to the card's **Notes** ("Rome trip: café and train went well; stuck on asking for the bill").
