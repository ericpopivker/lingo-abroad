# Practice Mode (Anki-style review)

Read this when the learner asks to practice, review, or "quiz me". Rules from SKILL.md (polite register, voice, memory tricks, progress card) still apply.

Free practice between lessons. **Only items from lessons the learner has done** (plus the current lesson); never new material, and **never your own new phrases or questions**.

**Direction follows the item's tag on the card:** **(S)** speak items → the learner **produces** them ("How do you say…?", situations, roleplay lines). **(L)** listen and **(R)** read items → **recognition only**: say or show the target-language line and ask "What does it mean?". Never ask the learner to say an (L) or (R) item, or to build a question from one. Untagged item: check the lesson spec it came from. Polite register, memory hooks, the English safety net and "slower" work as in lessons.

| Learner says | What you do |
| :----------- | :---------- |
| **"Practice"** / "practice everything" / "review" / "practice 5 minutes" | **Card review** (below) of **all** learned items: Weak first, then Learning oldest-first, plus an occasional Solid item. Default length: about 10 cards or 5 minutes |
| **"Practice the last lesson"** / "practice last lesson" | Card review of items from the **most recent lesson only** (all its Speak, Listen and Read items, missed ones twice) |
| **"Practice speaking / listening / signs"** | Card review with only Speak, Listen or Read items |
| **"Practice roleplay"** (optionally "…at a café / bakery / shop") | A free roleplay using **only learned phrases**; you play a polite local; correct informal or wrong phrases gently and mark them Weak |
| **"Quiz me on <topic>"** | Unscored warm-up: 5–6 items from that topic's lessons (no pass/fail; it isn't the topic test) |

**Card review, one card at a time (like Anki):**

1. **Front:** show or say the prompt only, using one of the **question types** below (rotate them).
2. **Learner answers first.** Never reveal before they try (in voice, wait; if they say "show me" or stay silent, reveal).
3. **Back:** say the correct answer (with respelling in text mode) and, if they missed it, the memory hook in one sentence.
4. **Move the item:** right → up one bucket (Weak → Learning → Solid); missed → Weak. The learner may override like Anki: **"again"** keeps it Weak, **"easy"** moves it straight to Solid.
5. Next card. Never repeat the same card twice in a row; a missed card comes back once later in the same session.

**Question types** (all work in voice; describe signs aloud; examples are Italian, use the target language):

| Item | Types |
| :--- | :---- |
| **Speak** (phrases the learner says) | **Situation**: "You walk into a shop at six in the evening. What do you say?" · **Translate**: "How do you say 'this one, please'?" · **Respond to a local**: you say _"Prego?"_ as a baker → learner answers · **Fill the gap**: "_Grazie_ …?" (_mille_) · **Polite fix**: "A friend taught you _Ciao_. What do you say to the shopkeeper when leaving?" |
| **Listen** (what locals say) | **What did they mean?**: say the local line → learner gives the meaning · **Natural speed**: the same line said quickly, like a real local ("slower" is allowed) · **What do you answer?**: say the local line → learner replies in the language |
| **Read** (signs, menus) | **Sign meaning**: "A sign on the ticket machine says _Guasto_." · **Opposites / pairs**: "If _Ingresso_ is the entrance, what's the exit?" · **Act on it**: "The door says _Spingere_. What do you do?" (only for learned signs) |
| **Any** | **Which one?** (two options, good in voice): "It's 7 p.m.: _Buongiorno_ or _Buonasera_?" · **Speed round**: 5 quick prompts in a row, no hooks · **Mini-dialogue**: a 3-line exchange where the learner fills in their lines · **Make your own hook**: "Make up your own way to remember _Permesso_" (save it in Notes) · **Say it after me**: pronunciation repeat (not scored) |

**Rotation rules:**

- **Vary the type:** never ask the same item the same way twice in a row; across sessions, rotate types for each item.
- **Match difficulty to the bucket:** **Weak** → easier types first (_Which one?_, _Fill the gap_, _Translate_), then a _Situation_; **Learning** → _Situation_, _What did they mean?_, _Sign meaning_; **Solid** → harder types (_Respond to a local_, _Natural speed_, _Mini-dialogue_, _Polite fix_).
- **Mix parts:** alternate Speak, Listen and Read items rather than grouping them (unless the learner asked to practice one part).
- **One speed round per session at most**, and **at most one** _Make your own hook_ or _Say it after me_ per session.
- _Say it after me_ and _Make your own hook_ don't move buckets.

**Ending:** stop when the learner says "stop" or the time/card count is reached. Say one sentence ("8 of 10 right; *binario* moved to Solid"), then update progress: print the card (practice **counts for the streak**, updates buckets and accuracy, but doesn't complete lessons or tests) (log row Type `practice` if asked).

**Places for roleplays:** never the same place twice in a row, and only places from topics the learner has done (after Transport: stations and ticket offices too, not just cafés and bakeries).
