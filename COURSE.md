# What you'll learn

**Level 1: Survival.** Everything you need for a first trip: 6 topics, 3 lessons each, about 7 minutes a lesson. Every lesson has the same four parts: phrases **you say** (always the polite form), what **locals say back**, the **signs** you'll see, and a short **roleplay** where your tutor plays the local. Each topic ends with a short test.

Take them in order, or jump to any lesson ("Restaurant lesson 2").

## 1. Basics

1. **Greetings:** say hello and goodbye politely in any shop, café or hotel
2. **Please and thank you:** ask for things by pointing, thank people, answer a thank-you
3. **Polite basics:** yes, no and OK; get someone's attention; get past people politely

## 2. Understanding & Getting Help

1. **When you don't understand:** ask if they speak English, ask them to slow down or repeat; read the signs for information, restrooms, women and men
2. **Working it out:** ask someone to write it down, how to say something, what a word means
3. **Getting help:** find the restroom and the pharmacy, call for help, ask for the police or a doctor

## 3. Transport

1. **Taxi:** give the address, ask the price, pay, and stop where you want
2. **Bus & metro:** buy a ticket, check the line goes where you want, know where to get off
3. **Train:** buy a one-way or return ticket, find the platform, make sure it's the right train

## 4. Sightseeing

1. **Finding your way:** ask where a sight is and whether it's far; understand straight ahead, left and right
2. **Tickets & opening hours:** buy tickets or show your booking, ask when it opens, spot the closing day
3. **At the sight:** ask about photos, get someone to take your picture, find the exit, follow church and museum rules

## 5. Restaurant

1. **Ordering:** get a table, order food and drinks, answer the waiter's usual questions
2. **Menu & dietary needs:** ask what a dish is, get a recommendation, say what you can't eat
3. **The bill:** compliment the food, ask for the bill, pay, understand what's on it

## 6. Shopping

1. **How much:** count to ten, ask what things cost, understand the price said back
2. **Finding things:** ask if a shop has something, where to buy it, and where the nearest ATM is
3. **Paying & deciding:** browse without pressure, decide to buy, pay by card or cash, spot "cash only"

## Along the way

- **Practice:** a few minutes on the phrases you find hardest, whenever you like
- **Freestyle:** after a few lessons, a free conversation in the language using what you know
- **Your own phrases:** ask "how do I say…?" anytime; your phrases get saved and practiced too
- **Progress card:** at the end of every lesson, a short summary of where you are and what to review next

**Coming later:** Level 2 (more detail on each topic, plus hotels, health and solving problems) and Level 3 (real conversation).
